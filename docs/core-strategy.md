# Core strategy: the deal sequence

This is the strategy behind every pitch in this repo. The full version, with meeting briefs, the 12-week calendar and fallbacks, is in `getluckyjo/simsoftware`: `docs/deal-strategy.md`, rendered as `docs/Get-Lucky-Deal-Sequence.pdf`.

## Six moves, one story
1. **Golfzon.** Sell the bays to get the partnership. The 272-bay programme in Virgin Active and Planet Fitness gets us Golfzon's global head. We leave with chain pricing, a reference design and talks on **Get Lucky mode** inside Golfzon's software.
2. **Planet Fitness (and Virgin Active).** Sell the concept: golf zones at zero capex, financed by RMB, run by Get Lucky, backed by Ernie Els.
3. **RMB.** Sell the finance on the concessions, the Golfzon quote, the bay model and the Santam-insured shot.
4. **Icons.** Sell the R10 million hole-in-one at Icons Cup South Africa (Fancourt, 11–13 Dec), fully underwritten.
5. **Santam.** Sell the global book: the Ernie-backed app, running globally on Golfzon, launched on the Icons broadcast.
6. **Icons Cup: the close.** Sign Ernie as founding partner at Fancourt, and announce Get Lucky going global: the app, integrated with Golfzon, plus Virgin Golf and Planet Golf bays.

## The principle
Nobody signs first on their own. Get the **conditional** yeses in order:
1. Golfzon pricing "subject to finance and concessions".
2. A Planet Fitness LOI "subject to finance and reference design".
3. An RMB indicative term sheet on the PF LOI plus the Golfzon quote.

Then close them together in January–February 2027.

## One story, told the same way everywhere
- **Ernie Els:** not signed until Fancourt. Say it verbally only: "founding partner, being finalised". Keep him out of writing to Golfzon and RMB.
- **272 bays:** "the programme we're rolling out with Virgin Active and Planet Fitness". Never "the order" until it's signed.
- **RMB:** "asset finance structured with RMB". It is not credit-approved yet.
- **R10m cover:** "in underwriting with Santam Specialist Solutions". Get a parallel contingency-market quote as a back-up, because Icons has been told the prize is secured.
