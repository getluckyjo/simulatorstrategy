# Planet Fitness CEO meeting: one-pager (internal, not for sending)
Updated 2 Oct 2026 with Golfzon's 1 Oct proposal. Model: `model/model_pf.py` (re-run).

## The ask
Planet Golf: 72 Golfzon bays in 42 clubs, 2027–2029. We buy and operate the bays on RMB asset finance. Planet Fitness provides the room and members, takes **20% of bay revenue**, no capex. Leave the meeting with: (1) agreement to a Sandton Phase 1 pilot, (2) the club list and room specs, (3) a named operations lead.

## What changed since the last Planet Fitness pitch
- **Golfzon confirmed as technology partner and sent a proposal (1 Oct).** Call with CEO Sean Pyun and team held 29 Sep.
- **Real hardware prices (USD, Phase 1 units 1–40):** Motion Plate 43k, Dual Plate 35k, Basic 30k; cheaper from unit 41 (40k / 32k / 25k). Software licence $99/month per system. Shipping, duties and booth build excluded. We now use Phase 1 prices for all 72 bays (conservative).
- Signature bay is now the Motion Plate (was budgeted at $60k): capex per Signature bay falls from R1.44m to R1.0m. Practice bay is now the Basic unit at $30k (was $22k, different product), so slightly dearer.
- Golfzon will run competitions: Golfzon Tour, monthly challenges, Leadbetter Connect remote coaching, Golfzon Global App. Our Hole-in-One Challenge is phased in from day one.
- Interest also from Virgin Active (200 bays, 130 clubs). Combined programme 272 bays. Golfzon's volume tiers favour the combined order.

## Why they say yes
- Zero capex, 20% of every rand, no operating burden. About R14m a year, roughly 1.4% on top of R1bn revenue.
- Retention and joiners: Golfzon claims indoor golf is social (98% of golfers don't play alone). Padel with Africa Padel already proved members book time for a game.
- Founders decide: Manny Rivera (Group CEO) and Mannee de Wet (MD). Plans about five new clubs a year to 2030: the zone goes into every set of drawings.
- Proudly South African, decisions in Sandton and Cape Town.

## Likely objections
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Floor space is revenue-generating" | Rooms are 2–5 bays. Planet Fitness gets 20% of bay revenue plus a joiner and retention story. Pilot at 2 clubs before committing. |
| "Will golf members cannibalise gym time?" | Bookable bays are a separate room, not gym floor. Peak gym and bay demand differ. |
| "What if it does not work?" | We carry the capex and the risk. Pilot first; exit terms to agree (removal at our cost). |
| "Why 20%?" | Opening position. Grid above shows we can go to 25% and still cover debt at 2.36x. |
| "Who services it?" | Golfzon support plus local technician. Golfzon proposal does not yet state SA service, warranty or lead times: open item, do not promise dates. |
| "Why not buy it ourselves?" | Possible, but they would carry R1m+ a bay and run it. Our structure is cheaper for them. |
