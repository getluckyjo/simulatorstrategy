Planet Fitness CEO meeting

Open with this

Manny, every Planet Fitness club already has your members; what none of them has is a reason to come on the nights they would otherwise skip. We want to give you that: Golfzon golf bays, the world's biggest commercial indoor golf network, in your clubs, booked like padel. Members play friends across your clubs and around the world, take lessons from Leadbetter coaches, get instant swing analysis, and their kids get their own junior tour. It costs Planet Fitness nothing: we buy the bays on asset finance and run them, and you take 20% of every rand, about R14 million a year at maturity. We start with two bays in Sandton in 2027 and put the zone in the drawings of every club you open after that.

The deal

72 Golfzon bays in 42 clubs, 2027–2029, on RMB asset finance. Planet Fitness gives the room and the members and takes 20% of bay revenue, with no capex. Leave with a Sandton pilot agreed, club and room specs, and a named ops lead. Two founders decide: Manny Rivera and Mannee de Wet.

Community benefits for members

  • Golfzon Tour. 130+ venues in 15 countries, $300k purse
  • Network Play. Live games club to club and worldwide, with video chat
  • Monthly Challenges. Golfzon-funded prizes, like US Open access
  • Inter-club league.* Run by a Golfzon Competitions Director
  • Golfzon Global App. Book, track progress, invite friends
  • Swing analysis. HD cameras, instant feedback, stance sensing
  • Kids club. Leadbetter Junior Tour; winners train with David Leadbetter
  • Leadbetter Connect. Live coaching through the sim, in off-peak hours
  • Get Lucky Hole-in-One Challenge. Insured shot from day one, plus leagues and corporate days

Likely pushback

  • "Floor space earns money." Separate room; they take 20%
  • "What if it fails?" Our capex and risk; we remove the bays at our cost
  • "Why 20%?" Opening position; at 25% debt cover is still 2.36x
  • "Who services it?" Golfzon and a local technician; SA service tbc
