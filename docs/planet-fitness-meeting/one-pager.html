Planet Fitness CEO meeting

Open with thisManny, every Planet Fitness club already has your members; what none of them has is a reason to come on the nights they would otherwise skip. We want to give you that: Golfzon golf bays, the world's biggest commercial indoor golf network, in your clubs, booked like padel. Members play friends across your clubs and around the world, take lessons from Leadbetter coaches, get instant swing analysis, and their kids get their own junior tour. It costs Planet Fitness nothing: we buy the bays on asset finance and run them, and you take 20% of every rand, about R14 million a year at maturity. We start with two bays in Sandton in 2027 and put the zone in the drawings of every club you open after that.

The ask

72 Golfzon bays in 42 clubs, 2027–2029. We buy and operate on RMB asset finance; Planet Fitness gives the room and members, takes 20% of bay revenue, no capex. Leave with a Sandton pilot agreed, club and room specs, and a named ops lead. Two founders decide: Manny Rivera and Mannee de Wet.
