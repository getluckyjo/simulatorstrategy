## Partnership structure: Hole-in-One Challenge integration
1. **Phase 1 – Hole in One Challenge:** from day one Golfzon activates existing TwoVisionNX competition features to run the Get Lucky indoor Hole-in-One Challenge.
2. **Phase 2 – Featured Partner:** Get Lucky promoted on the Golfzon Global App; branding on the hole in-game (full-proposal deck says "in-game branding – just like an outdoor golf course, now broadcast to our global audience"; pricing deck says "branding on the hole").
3. **Phase 3 – Full Integration:** the Challenge is fully embedded in Golfzon game settings, integral to the player experience.
