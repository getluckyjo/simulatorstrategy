Chapter one

South Africa.

Virgin Active's largest territory: 136 clubs, 623,000 members, 35% of group revenue. Two bays in a standard club, four in a Collection club, booked like a padel court. Ernie Els on the door.

The proposal goes to Virgin Active this quarter. We walk in with a platform, a reference design and a price. We would like the platform to be Golfzon.

  • Virgin Active has already started.Its first Collection Country Club, opened by Sir Richard Branson in Cape Town in February, includes a golf simulator. More Collection clubs follow in 2026.
  • Its owner needs a yield story.Brait is preparing to list or sell Virgin Active within two years. A golf zone in every club is a yield product.
  • The clock is running.Virgin Active UK is installing Trackman this year. Whoever sets the standard first sets it for the group.
