The concept

Chapter one: South Africa.

Virgin Active is the largest health-club operator in Africa: 136 clubs across South Africa, Botswana and Namibia and 623,000 active members. It is Virgin Active's biggest territory in the world, ahead of Italy and the UK. And in February 2026 it put a golf simulator into its first Collection Country Club, opened by Sir Richard Branson in Cape Town.

Virgin Golf by Ernie Els takes that idea to every club: two bays in a standard club, four in a Collection club, booked the way members book a padel court. South Africa's most famous golfer on the door, and the country's most established insurer, Santam, behind the brand that runs it.

— Johannes le Roux, Founder & MD, Get Lucky Golf Club

  • The operator is ready for it.Virgin Active calls its clubs "social wellness clubs" and is moving upmarket. The Collection tier already includes padel, recovery and a golf simulator, with more Collection upgrades planned across South Africa in 2026.
  • The owner needs a yield story.Brait, which owns 80% of Virgin Active, told the market in June 2026 that it will list or sell the business within two years. Southern African revenue is growing on yield from refurbished clubs. A golf zone in every club is a yield product.
  • Ernie makes it a brand, not a bay.Four majors, two U.S. Opens, a South African. Virgin Golf by Ernie Els gives every club a name members recognise before they walk in.
  • The proposal goes to Virgin Active in Q4 2026.Our platform partner is chosen before then, so that we walk in with a reference design, a price and a name. We would like that name to be Golfzon.

The order

What we are buying.

Two hundred simulators over three years, in three kinds of bay. Signature bays in the Collection clubs, play bays in the metro clubs, practice bays where the floor plan asks for them. One software platform across the chain so that a member in Sandton can play a member in Umhlanga on the same course on the same night.

  • Signature baysThe full experience for the Collection clubs: moving swing plate, multi-surface mat, the course library with Pebble Beach and St Andrews. Our assumption is TwoVision NX.
  • Play baysThe volume bay for a standard club: a two-bay zone that books by the hour, runs leagues and hosts a Friday night. TwoVision or Vision Standard.
  • Practice baysA range bay for coaching and drills, tied to Golfzon Leadbetter content. GDR Plus.

The vision

Chapter two: the world.

Virgin Active is one brand in eight countries: South Africa, the UK, Italy, Australia, Singapore, Thailand, Namibia and Botswana. Around 230 clubs and 1.2 million members, run by one group with one membership that already works across borders. Virgin Golf by Ernie Els is designed for all of it.

South Africa proves it: the largest territory, the home market of the name on the door, a Collection club that already has a simulator, and an owner preparing a listing. Then the same golf zone, on the same platform, goes to London, Milan, Sydney, Singapore and Bangkok. A member at Virgin Active Sandton plays a member at Virgin Active Chiswick on the same course, on the same night.

That is why the platform decision is a group decision, and why we want to make it once. Two Trackman bays are going into Chiswick this year. Whoever sets the standard in the first territory sets it for the group. We would like that to be Golfzon, and we would like the first territory to be ours.

Why Golfzon

The platform we want on the door next to Ernie's name.

We have looked at the market. Golfzon is the only platform that is at once the world's largest, the official simulator of the U.S. Open, and already selling into gyms and fitness centres. What it does not yet have is a global health-club group as a reference customer. We would like to be that, one territory at a time, starting with the largest.

The U.S. Open's simulator

Official indoor simulator of the U.S. Open and U.S. Women's Open. Our founding partner won the U.S. Open twice. That sentence sells itself in every club.

Thirteen thousand venues

The most installed commercial platform in the world, Golf Digest's best simulator seven years running, 240-plus courses, and a moving swing plate nobody else matches at scale.

A team in Virginia

Golfzon's Global Business Division moved to Fairfax County in March 2026. A new territory is exactly the decision that office exists to make, and we will come to it.

Gyms are already a Golfzon vertical

Golfzon sells to gyms and fitness centres today. A 136-club chain with 623,000 members is the case study those sales pages are missing.

Leadbetter is in the group

Coaching is half of a gym's golf product. Golfzon Leadbetter content in the practice bays makes Virgin Golf a place to get better, not just a place to play.

Before the standard is set elsewhere

Virgin Active's UK clubs are installing Trackman this year. If the group standard is set in London it will not be Golfzon. We would like it set in Cape Town, and carried to the other seven countries from there.
