Chapter one

South Africa.

Virgin Active's largest territory: 136 clubs, 623,000 members, 35% of group revenue. Two bays in a club, one in the smaller clubs, booked like a padel court. Ernie Els on the door.

The proposal goes to Virgin Active this quarter. We walk in with a platform, a reference design and a price. We would like the platform to be Golfzon.

  • Virgin Active has already started.Its first Collection Country Club, opened by Sir Richard Branson in Cape Town in February, includes a golf simulator. More Collection clubs follow in 2026.
  • The members are already there.623,000 members walk past the bay on every visit, and 139,000 handicapped golfers want a winter and weeknight home for their game.
  • South Africa is the proving ground.Virgin Active's largest territory and Ernie's home market. The numbers from 200 bays here carry the golf zone to the rest of the group.
