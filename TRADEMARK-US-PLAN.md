# GET LUCKY — United States trade mark plan (Class 41 first)

**Get Lucky Golf Club (Pty) Ltd · 3 October 2026 · working plan, not legal advice**

Context: a South African application for GET LUCKY has preliminary acceptance. South Africa is not
in the Madrid system, so the US is a direct national filing at the USPTO. Foreign-domiciled
applicants must be represented by a US-licensed attorney for every filing.

## Quality rules that save money

- Services wording from the ID Manual, nothing custom: avoids $200 per class in surcharges and the most common office action.
- One applicant name, matching the SA record and the company registration; mismatches cost an amendment.
- A US address is not needed, but a domicile address for the company and an attorney of record are.
- Keep every specimen dated and in its original form from day one of the pilot; recreated specimens get refused.
- Do not file the logo until the words have cleared examination; the logo inherits the same refusals at twice the cost.

## What to send the attorney in the first email

The SA application number, filing date and the acceptance letter; the mark exactly as used; the list of classes and services above; the company's registration details; and the note that the applicant is foreign-domiciled and the services launch in the US in January 2027.
