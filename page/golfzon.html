PartOne

The order: Virgin Golf.

Golfzon already sells to gyms and fitness centres. Nobody has sold a whole national chain. We think the first one is in South Africa, and we think we can open the door.

Why Virgin Active, and why now

  • It is the biggest health-club operator in Africa — 136 clubs, 623,000 active members, and the largest of Virgin Active's nine territories by revenue (35%, ahead of Italy and the UK).
  • It has already put a golf simulator in its flagship. Sir Richard Branson opened the first Collection Country Club at Point, Green Point, Cape Town, in February 2026: 5,500 m², a $5m-plus refit, padel, a recovery suite and a golf simulator — with more Collection upgrades planned across South Africa in 2026.
  • Its owner needs a yield story. Brait (80%, Christo Wiese; Virgin Group 20%) told the market in June 2026 it will list or sell Virgin Active within two years, raising $147m to get there. Southern African revenue grew 6% on yield as clubs were refurbished. A golf zone in every club is exactly the premium-yield product that story needs.
  • Trackman is already in Virgin Active's UK clubs. Two Trackman iO bays open at Chiswick Riverside this year. If the group standard is set in London, it will be Trackman. We would like it set in Cape Town, on Golfzon.
  • Golf is South Africa's club sport. 460-plus golf clubs and 139,000 handicapped club members; the Vitality wellness programme already gives its members Virgin Active access; and the country's most famous golfer is our founding partner.

What Virgin Golf is

A branded golf zone in every Virgin Active club, built on Golfzon: two bays in a standard club, three to four in a Collection club, with Golfzon's course library, Network Play across the chain, coaching through Golfzon Leadbetter content, a member ladder, and the Get Lucky challenge on the designated hole of every course. Members book it the way they book a padel court. Non-members buy it the way they buy a day pass.

PartTwo

The game: the insured hole-in-one, in your software, everywhere.

Golfzon's own data says a simulator ace happens once every 6,992 par-3 swings. That is the number that makes this insurable, and it is why the moment should be sold, not given away.

How it works in the bay

  • One designated Get Lucky hole per course, chosen by us and built into Golfzon's software. The player never picks the hole, so the "lucky hole" problem Korea knows well goes away, and the insurer gets a measured rate per hole.
  • The slip: $1 → $500, $2 → $1,000, $5 → $2,500. One entry per round. Bought at booking, at the bay screen, or in the league.
  • The Million Dollar Shot: $1,000 → one flat $1,000,000 on a designated 170-yard-plus hole. Amateurs only — PGA members, tour players and anyone who has earned professional prize money are excluded, declared against ID. One attempt a round, three a day. Flagship venues and events first.
  • Your telemetry is the verifier. Ball-in-hole event, launch data, session and member ID, timestamp; bay video where it exists. Golfzon already auto-captures the ace video for Carrot Insurance in Korea. Settings locked for insured attempts; no practice-mode entries.
  • The insurer pays the winner directly. Get Lucky and Golfzon are never in the prize funds flow. Ladder claims confirmed in 24 hours; the Million Dollar Shot on a documented protocol.

Why the number is safe to say out loud. The National Hole-in-One Registry puts the average golfer at 1 in 12,000 outdoors. An insurer can price that. Nobody in the world sells the jackpot on it.

Why Golfzon, and why not somebody else

You own the platform, the U.S. Open, the Tour and the ace video pipe. You do not own the moment yet.

  • Scale nobody matches: 9,400-plus locations, 47,000-plus simulators, 41 countries, 5.5 million members, 100 million rounds a year.
  • The U.S. Open's official simulator — and our founding partner won it twice. That sentence sells itself in every bay.
  • The Global Business Division moved to Virginia in March 2026. Every market outside Korea is now decided in the US, where 19 million people played only off-course last year and there are 1,500-plus commercial simulator venues.
  • Korea proves the demand. Five insurers and Golfzon itself sell screen-golf ace cover at $0.75–$1.20 a round, and the ace-to-insurer video pipe exists because Golfzon built it for Carrot. Every one of those products pays $112–$224 of celebration costs. None of them pays a jackpot.
  • The white space is closing. Full Swing's Skill Strike pays $100 for a $3 ace and Full Swing now belongs to Golf Channel's owner. Five Iron sells $1 closest-to-pin entries on Trackman. On Golfzon there is no per-shot money game at all. We would like to be it, first and exclusively.

What the challenge does for Golfzon

It is not a revenue line by itself — at a 10% attach the slip earns a bay a few hundred dollars a year. It is the reason to walk into the bay, the video every ace produces, and a story the Tour and Network Play can carry: a live Million Dollar Shot with Ernie Els, monthly, on a Golfzon screen. The revenue is in the bays it fills and the chains it helps sell — Virgin Active first.

In the US, our base case has the challenge on ~1,500 paid bays by the 2027–28 winter across Golfzon, Five Iron and X-Golf, with operators sharing about $340k in year one and $680k in year two, plus the Million Dollar Shot at flagship venues. Golfzon's share scales with Golfzon's bays; category exclusivity on the platform is on the table for a minimum venue count.

Legal, in one paragraph. In South Africa we hold a written skill-based opinion and a bound underwriter. In the US, hole-in-one contests for an entry fee have been held to be skill contests since Nevada in 1961; we have mapped all fifty states, will run paid entry in the 18 states where the answer is clear (New York, Illinois, Texas, Florida, California and Georgia among them) and a free-to-play, operator-funded version everywhere else, with counsel's opinion before any term sheet. Korea, later, as an insurance-wrapped product with a Korean carrier and Santam Re behind it — the structure Carrot × Golfzon already uses.
