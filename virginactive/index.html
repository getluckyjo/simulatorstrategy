The offer

We bring the bays. You bring the members.

A ten-year exclusive concession. Get Lucky Golf buys, installs and runs the bays. RMB finances them. Virgin Active provides the room and the members, and takes 20% of everything the bays earn.

Virgin Active

Puts in

Two bays (60 m²) in 70 clubs, one bay (30 m²) in 60 smaller clubs, inside planned refurbishments. Booking in the Virgin Active app. Marketing to members. Ten-year exclusive.

Gets out

R37 million a year at maturity. No capital, no operating risk. A golf add-on on the member bill. Ernie Els on the door. First call on the UK, Italy, Australia and Asia.

Get Lucky Golf

Puts in

The bays, the equity, Golfzon, the Ernie Els brand, coaching, leagues, the insured shot, and a golf host in every Collection club. We run it.

Gets out

An operating business of 200 bays, and the reference case for the rest of the Virgin Active group.

RMB

Puts in

Asset finance for the equipment, drawn in three phases, secured over the bays and the concession.

Gets out

A financed order the bays repay from bookings. Virgin Active never carries the debt.

Two terms are yours to negotiate.

We open at 20% of bay revenue, with Virgin Active building the shell inside its refurbishments. Both posts move. A bigger share, or us building the shell, still works for us and for RMB. We would rather fit the deal than hold a number.
